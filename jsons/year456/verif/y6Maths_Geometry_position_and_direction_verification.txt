2.  Assume (0,0) and (3,0) are opposite vertices. The midpoint of this diagonal is ((0+3)/2, (0+0)/2) = (1.5, 0). The third given vertex (0,4) must be an endpoint of the other diagonal.     -   (0,0) to (0,4): Length = 4
    -   (0,4) to (3,-4): Length = sqrt((3-0)^2 + (-4-4)^2) = sqrt(9 + 64) = sqrt(73)
